# Code of Conduct

Raids and Gear is a game about scavengers who survive by covering each other. Our
community runs the same way. This is the behavior standard for **every official space** —
this repo (Issues, Discussions), the Discord server, and anywhere carrying the Raids and
Gear name.

## The short version

Be the squadmate you'd want covering your six. Argue about loot tables, not about people.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good faith. Critique ideas, code, and balance — not the person.
- Keep feedback specific and actionable. "This biome feels unfair" is a start; "Sky Rig
  boss enrage out-DPSes a full squad at wave 3, repro seed 8412" is gold.
- Welcome newcomers. Everyone was new to iso shooters, to Solana, or to both.
- Respect privacy and keep threads on-topic.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, hate speech, slurs, or discrimination of any kind.
- Threats, intimidation, stalking, or sustained disruption.
- Doxxing — posting anyone's private information without consent.
- Sexual or violent content, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Scams, phishing, and impersonation — the fastest route to a permanent ban here.

## Crypto-specific rules (read these)

A token-backed game attracts predators. These are zero-tolerance:

- **No staff member will ever DM you first, and no one will ever ask for your seed phrase
  or private key.** Anyone who does is a scammer — report and block them.
- There is exactly **one** token (`$RNG`) and **one** official domain (`raidsandgear.com`).
  "Claim", "airdrop", "double your RNG", "connect your wallet to unlock", and look-alike
  links are **scams**. Posting them is an instant ban.
- **No impersonation** of the team, moderators, or other members.
- **No price / financial-advice spam.** `$RNG` is a game token, not an investment vehicle.

## Scope

Applies in all official spaces and when representing the project in public. It applies
equally to community members, contributors, moderators, and the core team — rank does not
exempt anyone.

## Enforcement

Moderators enforce proportionally:

| Severity                            | Typical response                 |
| ----------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Minor / first-time break            | Private warning; content removed |
| Repeated or moderate                | Temporary timeout / mute         |
| Severe or persistent                | Ban                              |
| Scams, doxxing, threats, NSFW, hate | **Immediate ban**, no warning    |

Bans for non-severe causes are appealable to a second moderator who was not involved.

## Reporting

- **This repo / Discussions:** flag the content and email **[community@raidsandgear.com](mailto:community@raidsandgear.com)**.
- **Discord:** DM a moderator or use the server's report tooling.
- **Security vulnerabilities are not conduct reports** — follow [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).

Reports are handled discreetly. We will not retaliate against good-faith reporters and
will protect reporter identity to the extent we can.
