# Raids and Gear — Community & Feedback

Welcome, operator. This is the public home for **bug reports, feedback, ideas, and
discussion** about Raids and Gear — the isometric action-RPG where you raid, loot, and
**own** what you earn on-chain.

> The game's source code is private. **This** repo is where the community and the team
> meet: file issues, talk in Discussions, and watch the public roadmap.

When you file a bug, the single most useful thing is a **repro**: biome + procgen seed
(shown in the HUD) + what you did + what happened. **Never** share your wallet's seed
phrase or private key — nobody on the team will ever ask for it.

## How feedback turns into change

Everything actionable becomes one tracked issue. We triage within ~2 business days,
decide weekly (accept / not-now / no — always with a reason), and when something ships
we point it back at whoever raised it and credit them in a biweekly "you said / we did"
digest. One backlog, in the open.

## The rules

- Be the squadmate you'd want covering your six. Read the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
- Heads up on scams: **staff never DM you first and never ask for your seed phrase.**
  There is one token (`$RNG`) and one official site (`raidsandgear.com`). Anything
  promising free claims, airdrops, or "connect to unlock" is a scam — report it.
