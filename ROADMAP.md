# Roadmap

The live roadmap is the **[Projects board](https://github.com/orgs/Raids-and-Gear/projects)**
for this repo. It's public on purpose: you can see what we've heard, what we've committed
to, and what's shipped.

## How issues flow

| Column          | Meaning                                                                    |
| --------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Triage**      | New, acknowledged, not yet decided. We get here within ~2 business days.   |
| **Backlog**     | Accepted but not scheduled yet. Carries a one-line "why not now."          |
| **In progress** | Being worked on.                                                           |
| **Shipped**     | Live. Linked from patch notes and the biweekly "you said / we did" digest. |

Items we won't do are closed with a **respectful, public reason** rather than left to rot
— a clear "no" beats silence.

## What's not here

Exact dates and unreleased content specifics. We share direction, not a contract: a
roadmap is a statement of intent that changes as we learn from your feedback. Big beats
(new biomes, co-op scoring, seasons) get announced in
[Discussions → Announcements](https://github.com/Raids-and-Gear/issues/discussions)
and on Discord.

## Want to influence it?

[File an issue](https://github.com/Raids-and-Gear/issues/issues/new/choose) or
join a [Discussion](https://github.com/Raids-and-Gear/issues/discussions). The
items that ship are overwhelmingly the ones the community made a clear, well-evidenced
case for.
